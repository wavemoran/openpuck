Restore your puck

Before you start

This writes your backup file back to the puck. It takes about 2 minutes.

  • This puts the puck back exactly as it was when you made the backup. That includes the original software, and it replaces OpenPuck.
  • Restoring is at your own risk.
  • Use only a backup made from this puck. Keep the backup private.
  • Don't unplug the puck while it is being written.
